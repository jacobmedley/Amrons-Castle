# Collaborate respectfully

You are welcome to participate regardless of background or experience. Keep discussion focused on the project, explain disagreements with evidence, and respect other people's time and boundaries.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share another person's private information or use sexualized language toward contributors. Do not post private account records or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. If you need help with a conduct concern, request a private contact channel from a repository maintainer without posting personal details publicly. No dedicated confidential mailbox or response deadline is currently offered.
